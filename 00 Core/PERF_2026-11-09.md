# DED performance pass, re-applied and measured (2026-11-09)

The October design (`ded-performance-and-flextag.md`, 2026-10-03) was not in this build. It is now re-applied, together with fixes for the two frame spikes measured on 2026-11-08. Everything below was measured in OpenMW 0.52 RC1 with the real Morrowind, Tribunal and Bloodmoon data.

## What changed

| change | file | removes |
|---|---|---|
| NPCs have no `onUpdate`. Each poll arms the next one (`makeNpcDriver`): there is a generation counter, `onInactive` retires the chain, `onLoad` re-arms it, and a poll that raises re-arms before re-raising. One callback per chain, so a poll allocates no closure. | `common.lua`, `npc.lua` | the per-NPC, per-frame dispatch, which grows with frame rate |
| Two-tier polling. Every poll reads the equipped weapon, the shield, the stance and the settings version. The inventory is walked on every 4th poll, or at once when the cheap tier changes. | `common.lua` | 3/4 of the inventory walks |
| `count` is read only for ammo | `common.lua` | a read per weapon per walk, and rebuilds when a stack's count changes |
| Settings are delivered by version. `global.lua` bumps `version` after writing a change. Each actor compares it on its own poll and refreshes then. Nothing subscribes to the section. | `common.lua`, `global.lua` | the settings-change spike: 80 cache rebuilds used to run in one frame |
| An NPC's first poll lands at a random point in the first 0.5 s after it becomes active (`ACTIVATE_SPREAD`) | `common.lua` | most of DED's share of the activation spike |
| The filter sets are built on first use, never at module load and never for the player | `common.lua` | part of both spikes |

The player keeps a per-frame `onUpdate`. It is one script, and it walks its inventory on every poll, so a pickup shows within 0.5 s.

**Trade-offs:**
- Gear can appear up to 0.5 s after a cell loads.
- A gear change that is not on the cheap tier can take up to 4 s to show on an NPC. Examples are picking up a sword or firing arrows.
- Drawing and sheathing still show on the next poll.

## Tests

- `test_poll.lua` was rewritten around a simulated simulation clock, with one module copy per NPC as in game. It has 39 checks:
  - activation spread;
  - one poll per interval;
  - one walk per 4 polls;
  - near-zero allocation per unchanged poll;
  - what rebuilds;
  - the display toggle;
  - the range gate;
  - one chain after repeated `onActive`/`onLoad`;
  - no polls or live timers after `onInactive`;
  - a chain surviving a raise;
  - 80 NPCs refreshing settings across one interval rather than one frame (worst frame 6 of 80).
- `test_filters.lua`: the integration half now drives the timer driver.
- `test_ied.lua`: `setCfg` bumps `version`, as `global.lua` now does.
- Results on Lua 5.4 and LuaJIT:
  - `test_poll`, `test_ied`, `test_povrefresh` and `test_animrefresh` pass on both.
  - `test_filters` fails the same 2 data checks it failed before: `ashlander` group, and redoran in the temple family. Those are unrelated to this pass.
- `luacheck` (17 files), `check_handlers`, `check_manifest` and `globalcheck` are clean. `check_load` flags the vendored `multiCheckbox.lua` GMST name, identically in the untouched build.

## In engine (3 alternating runs per configuration)

Lua ms per second, with DED's cost given as the difference from a baseline without DED that ran alternately with it:

| phase | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| 80 NPCs shown | +13.2 | **+9.4** |
| 80 NPCs, display off | +4.4 | +2.5 |
| 80 NPCs, out of range | +4.4 | +2.5 |
| 80 NPCs activating | +19.9 | +13.7 |
| 80 NPCs, settings changed | +17.0 | +11.6 |

The worst single frame, in ms:

| event | before | after | no DED |
|---|---|---|---|
| a settings change | 52.5 | **19.9** | 4.3 |
| 80 NPCs activating | 79.8 | **55.4** | 25.8 |

These runs are at about 10 fps under llvmpipe. The removed dispatch cost grows with frame rate, so the gain is larger on real hardware. Before, the projection at 60 fps was 18–26 ms/s. Now the cost does not depend on frame rate, which puts it at roughly 9 ms/s, about 0.16 ms per frame.

The remaining activation cost is DED's module bodies running once per NPC script. Deferring the work cannot remove that.

The probe (DED's own time, 20 s at 80 NPCs):

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| calls | 20 474 | 1 712 |
| DED's own time | 226 ms | 109 ms |
| display off | 33.4 ms | 11.2 ms |
| out of range | 50.6 ms | 16.5 ms |

With `wayn`'s loadout (10 weapons and a shield), DED's own time went from 265 ms to 125 ms.

In Vivec, St. Delyn (29 NPCs), DED stays within the noise between runs, as it did before.
